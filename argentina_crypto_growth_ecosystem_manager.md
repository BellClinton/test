# Crypto Growth & Ecosystem Manager (Argentina)

## Location
Buenos Aires, Argentina / Remote in Argentina

## Employment
Full-time

## About the role
We are looking for a Crypto Growth & Ecosystem Manager to drive user growth, ecosystem partnerships, and market expansion initiatives across Argentina. This role is designed for crypto-native operators who understand how products, communities, and liquidity come together in real markets.

This is not a generic marketing position. You will work across growth, partnerships, market intelligence, and ecosystem activation. The ideal candidate understands local crypto behavior in Argentina, knows how users move between exchanges, wallets, stablecoins, and on/off-ramp products, and can turn that understanding into practical growth initiatives.

## What you will do
- Build and execute growth initiatives focused on crypto user acquisition, activation, and retention in Argentina.
- Identify and develop partnerships with exchanges, wallet providers, fintechs, payment companies, communities, and ecosystem operators.
- Track local market behavior, including stablecoin demand, exchange flows, retail participation, and user pain points.
- Launch ecosystem campaigns tied to product adoption, referrals, liquidity usage, and community growth.
- Work closely with product, operations, and leadership teams to prioritize high-impact market opportunities.
- Translate market feedback into concrete go-to-market actions and product insights.
- Support local market expansion through partnerships, events, community initiatives, and strategic relationship-building.

## What we are looking for
- 3+ years of experience in crypto, growth, partnerships, community, business development, or go-to-market roles.
- Strong understanding of wallets, exchanges, stablecoins, on/off-ramp products, and core onchain user behavior.
- Good knowledge of the Argentina market and the macro conditions that drive crypto adoption.
- Ability to combine commercial thinking with hands-on execution.
- Comfortable working in fast-moving, ambiguous environments.
- Fluent Spanish and professional English.

## Nice to have
- Experience in Latin America markets beyond Argentina.
- Existing network across crypto communities, exchanges, wallets, fintechs, or ecosystem operators.
- Experience launching referral, community, ambassador, or partnership-led growth programs.
- Familiarity with crypto analytics, growth reporting, or user segmentation.

## Why this role is different
- This is not a pure marketing role.
- You will influence how crypto products grow in-market, which partnerships matter, and how ecosystem demand is converted into real user activity.
- The role combines ecosystem thinking, growth execution, market understanding, and relationship-building.

## Compensation

- Performance-based upside.
- Flexible remote setup in Argentina.
