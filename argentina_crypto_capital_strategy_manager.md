# Crypto Capital Strategy Manager (Argentina)

## Location
Buenos Aires, Argentina / Remote in Argentina

## Employment
Full-time

## About the role
We are looking for a Crypto Capital Strategy Manager to help shape and execute capital allocation, liquidity, and market expansion initiatives across Argentina. This is not a traditional investment role. You will work at the intersection of treasury strategy, crypto market operations, partnerships, and growth.

The ideal candidate understands how digital assets move through real markets, knows the local Argentina context, and can turn market insight into practical action. You will support initiatives around stablecoins, on/off-ramp flows, liquidity partners, yield opportunities, and ecosystem relationships.

## What you will do
- Build and manage capital deployment strategies across crypto products, liquidity channels, and ecosystem partners.
- Evaluate opportunities in stablecoin adoption, treasury optimization, and regional crypto infrastructure.
- Track local market dynamics in Argentina, including user behavior, exchange activity, FX pressure, and demand for dollar-linked digital assets.
- Work with leadership on new market initiatives tied to growth, capital efficiency, and strategic partnerships.
- Analyze potential partners such as exchanges, OTC desks, payment providers, wallet platforms, and fintech operators.
- Develop operating frameworks for liquidity planning, counterparty evaluation, and risk-aware capital allocation.
- Translate investment and market intelligence into concrete go-to-market actions.
- Coordinate with legal, compliance, and operations teams to ensure execution is practical and scalable.

## What we are looking for
- 3+ years of experience in crypto, fintech, capital markets, treasury, partnerships, or strategic finance.
- Strong understanding of digital asset markets, stablecoins, yield structures, and exchange or OTC workflows.
- Good knowledge of the Argentina market and the macro context that drives crypto adoption.
- Ability to evaluate opportunities commercially, not only financially.
- Strong analytical thinking with a hands-on operating mindset.
- Comfortable working in ambiguous, fast-moving environments.
- Fluent Spanish and professional English.

## Nice to have
- Experience working with Latin America markets beyond Argentina.
- Background in crypto trading operations, treasury, payments, or BD.
- Existing network across exchanges, fintechs, OTC desks, or crypto-native operators.
- Familiarity with compliance considerations around digital assets in emerging markets.

## Why this role is different
- This is not a pure investment analyst position.
- You will influence how capital is used, where liquidity should be built, and which partnerships can unlock market growth.
- The role combines strategy, market understanding, execution, and relationship-building.

## Compensation
- Competitive salary in USD or equivalent structure.
- Performance-based upside.
- Flexible remote setup in Argentina.

## How to apply
Send your CV or LinkedIn profile together with a short note explaining why Argentina is one of the most important crypto markets in the world and how you would approach capital strategy in this market.
