# Web3 Senior Growth & Ecosystem Lead — Singapore

## About the Role

Web3preneur is hiring a crypto-native growth operator based in Singapore to drive
ecosystem expansion across Southeast Asia. You will own partnerships, community
growth, protocol integrations, and revenue pipelines across one of the fastest
moving Web3 corridors in the world.

This is a senior role for someone who already operates at the intersection of
crypto capital, builder communities, institutional DeFi, and regulatory
conversations in APAC. You know the Singapore Web3 landscape — the funds, the
protocols, the exchanges, the founders, and the events that matter.

If you already sit at the center of crypto deal flow in Asia, and you want a
platform that gives you bigger reach, stronger positioning, and elite access
across global Web3 markets, this role is designed for you.

## Location

Singapore (on-site / hybrid), with travel to major APAC and global Web3 events
including TOKEN2049 Singapore, Korea Blockchain Week, ETHGlobal, Consensus,
Devcon, and private networking gatherings.
