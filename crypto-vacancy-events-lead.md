# Web3 Head of Events & Strategic Activations

## About the Role

Web3preneur is hiring a Head of Events & Strategic Activations to own and scale
our global event strategy. You will design, produce, and monetize premium Web3
experiences — from flagship summits and invite-only founder dinners to sponsored
side events at every major blockchain conference worldwide.

This is not an event coordinator role. This is a revenue-driving leadership
position for someone who knows how to turn a room full of crypto executives into
closed deals, paid sponsorships, and long-term ecosystem partnerships. You
understand that the best Web3 deals happen off-stage — at curated dinners,
private roundtables, and exclusive after-parties where trust is built face to
face.

If you have produced high-end crypto events, sold six-figure sponsorship
packages, and built a reputation as someone who creates rooms people want to be
in, this role gives you the platform and budget to do it at global scale.

## Location

Remote-first with mandatory travel to 10–15 major Web3 events per year.
Preferred time zones: UTC+0 to UTC+8 (Dubai, Europe, Singapore, or APAC).

## Compensation

- Base salary: USD 150,000 – 250,000 per year
- Performance bonus: up to USD 200,000 per year based on event revenue,
  sponsorship sales, attendee satisfaction, and partnership conversions
- Token/equity incentives for exceptional candidates
- Unlimited travel budget for Web3preneur-produced and partner events
- Production budget ownership for flagship and side events

## What You Will Do

- Own the full lifecycle of Web3preneur events globally: concept, curation,
  production, sponsorship sales, execution, and post-event monetization.
- Design and scale flagship properties: Web3preneur Summit, RWA Roundtables,
  Founder Dinners, Investor Salons, and Women in Web3 activations.
- Produce premium side events at TOKEN2049, Consensus, ETH Dubai, Devcon,
  Korea Blockchain Week, India Blockchain Month, and Singapore Blockchain Week.
- Sell and deliver sponsorship packages ranging from USD 25,000 to USD 500,000+
  to protocols, exchanges, funds, Layer 1/Layer 2 teams, and institutional
  players.
- Curate invite lists that balance founders, investors, exchange operators,
  protocol teams, KOLs, media, and strategic partners.
- Build a network of venue partners, production teams, catering contacts, and
  AV suppliers across Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Seoul, Lisbon,
  and New York.
- Create repeatable event formats that generate revenue, deepen ecosystem
  relationships, and position Web3preneur as the premium access layer in Web3.
- Track and report event ROI: revenue per event, cost per attendee, sponsor
  renewal rate, partnership conversion rate, and NPS.

## Who You Are

- You have produced premium crypto or tech events that people talk about.
- You understand that event quality is measured in deals closed, not headcount.
- You can sell a USD 100,000 sponsorship package as easily as you can brief a

- You thrive under pressure. Event days are long, chaotic, and high-stakes.
  You love it.

- Excellent English communication, both written and verbal.
