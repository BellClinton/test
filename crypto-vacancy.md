# Web3 Elite Network & Partnerships Director

## About the Role

Web3preneur is looking for a crypto-native operator who already lives inside the
Web3 ecosystem: founders, whales, investors, exchanges, protocols, funds, family
offices, market makers, launchpads, KOLs, and conference power circles.

This is not a junior community role. This is a high-visibility position for
someone who understands capital, status, reputation, access, and deal flow in
crypto. You will represent Web3preneur as a premium gateway into the Web3
ecosystem and help turn relationships into partnerships, revenue, investments,
events, grants, and strategic opportunities.

If your network is your strongest asset, if you know how serious crypto money
moves, and if you want a title that gives you a bigger stage, this role is built
for you.

## Location

Dubai, United Arab Emirates / Remote, with international travel for major Web3
conferences and private networking events.
