# Crypto Investment Products Specialist — Singapore

## About the Role

Web3preneur is looking for a Crypto Investment Products Specialist based in
Singapore to design, structure, and distribute digital asset investment products
across institutional and accredited investor channels in APAC.

You will sit at the intersection of crypto markets, product structuring, and
investor relations — building the investment vehicles, yield strategies, and
tokenized products that sophisticated capital allocators demand. This is a role
for someone who understands both traditional finance product architecture and
the native mechanics of DeFi, staking, restaking, liquid staking, structured
vaults, RWA tokenization, and on-chain fund infrastructure.

Singapore's MAS-regulated environment, deep institutional liquidity, and growing
appetite for compliant crypto exposure make this the ideal base for someone who
wants to shape how the next wave of capital enters digital assets.

If you think in terms of risk-adjusted returns, product-market fit for
institutional crypto, and regulatory-first distribution — and you already have
the network to place products — this role was built for you.

## Location

Singapore (on-site / hybrid). Proximity to MAS-regulated entities, family
offices, fund administrators, and institutional counterparties is essential.

## Compensation

- Base salary: SGD 180,000 – 300,000 per year
- Performance bonus: up to SGD 250,000 per year tied to AUM growth, product
  launches, distribution milestones, and investor onboarding
- Carry/token participation in products you help structure and launch
- Full travel budget for investor roadshows, conferences, and due diligence
  meetings across APAC and globally

## What You Will Do

- Design and structure crypto investment products: tokenized funds, yield
  strategies, structured notes, staking/restaking products, DeFi vault
  strategies, RWA-backed instruments, and hybrid CeFi/DeFi vehicles.
- Build distribution pipelines to Singapore-based family offices, accredited
  investors, crypto funds, wealth managers, private banks, and institutional
  allocators.
- Navigate MAS regulatory frameworks (Securities and Futures Act, Payment
  Services Act, VCC structures, Recognized Market Operators) to ensure
  products are compliant and distributable in Singapore.
- Work with legal, compliance, and fund administration partners to bring
  products from concept to market: term sheets, offering documents, smart
  contract architecture, custodial setups, and investor reporting.
- Develop product narratives and investor materials that translate complex
  crypto-native strategies into institutional-grade language.
- Monitor and analyze crypto market conditions, yield opportunities, protocol
  risks, and macro trends to inform product design and timing.
- Build relationships with custodians, prime brokers, OTC desks, exchanges,
  fund administrators, and audit firms in Singapore's digital asset ecosystem.
- Represent Web3preneur at investor meetings, roadshows, conferences, and
  private allocator events across APAC.

## Who You Are

- You have a deep understanding of both crypto-native yield/investment
  mechanisms and traditional fund/product structuring.
- You can explain a restaking strategy to a family office CIO and a VCC
  structure to a DeFi protocol team — in the same afternoon.
- You are fluent in the language of institutional capital: Sharpe ratios,
  drawdown profiles, NAV calculations, waterfall structures, and LP reporting.
- You are equally fluent in crypto-native mechanics: TVL, APY sources, smart
  contract risk, protocol governance, tokenomics, and on-chain analytics.
- You understand that compliance is a distribution advantage, not a barrier.
- You have relationships with allocators who are already in crypto or actively
  exploring exposure.
- You are precise, analytical, and detail-oriented — products that fail on
  risk management or documentation don't get distributed.

## Requirements

- 4+ years of experience in investment product design, structuring, fund
  management, wealth management, or institutional sales — with at least 2 years
  directly in crypto, digital assets, DeFi, or blockchain-based financial
  products.
- Strong understanding of crypto investment mechanisms: staking, liquid staking,
  restaking, DeFi yield strategies, tokenized securities, RWA products,
  structured products, and on-chain fund infrastructure.
- Working knowledge of Singapore's regulatory landscape for digital asset
  products: MAS frameworks, SFA, PSA, VCC structures, CIS regulations, and
  accredited/institutional investor exemptions.
- Demonstrated ability to raise capital, distribute products, or onboard
  institutional/accredited investors into crypto-related vehicles.
- Strong quantitative and analytical skills: financial modeling, risk analysis,
  portfolio construction, and product pricing.
- Based in Singapore with existing relationships in the local institutional
  and family office ecosystem.
- Excellent English communication; Mandarin a significant advantage for APAC
  investor coverage.

## Nice to Have

- Experience structuring Variable Capital Company (VCC) vehicles or Singapore

- Track record of launching or distributing a crypto investment product with
  USD 10M+ AUM.
